FAQ

Common questions.

What is WashPilot?
WashPilot is a digital POS and business management platform for car washes. It replaces handwritten ledgers and spreadsheets, and works on desktop and mobile.
Who is WashPilot for?
Independent car wash owners, small and medium car washes, multi-branch operators, first-time car wash entrepreneurs, and car washes moving away from manual records.
Do I need complicated software?
No. WashPilot is meant to give car wash businesses a digital way to manage their work without expensive or complicated software.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. WashPilot works on both desktop and mobile.
What is the difference between Free and Premium?
Free gives you access to basic platform functionality. Premium adds additional business features.
Will my car wash be listed publicly?
Only Premium accounts receive a public listing in the WashPilot facilities directory. Free accounts are not listed.
