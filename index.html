GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A few things to know before choosing your WashPilot plan.

What is WashPilot? ＋

WashPilot is a carwash management workspace for handling wash tickets, service queues, payments, customer and vehicle records, and business operations.

Is Basic really free? ＋

Yes. Basic is free forever for one branch. Upgrade to Standard for ₱499/month or Premium for ₱899/month as your operation grows.

Can I upgrade later? ＋

Yes. Start with Basic and request an upgrade whenever you need Standard or Premium. Paid-plan access begins after your payment has been reviewed and confirmed.

What is included in each plan? ＋

Basic includes essential POS, wash queue, services, add-ons, receipts, customer records and daily sales for one branch. Standard adds inventory, expenses, staff tools and advanced reports for one branch. Premium includes everything in Standard plus multiple branches and consolidated reporting.

Can my staff use WashPilot? ＋

Yes. The workspace includes focused roles for owners, cashiers, washers, and supervisors.
