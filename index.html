A few things to know before choosing your WashPilot plan.

More questions →
What is WashPilot?

WashPilot is a carwash management workspace for handling wash tickets, service queues, payments, customer and vehicle records, and day-to-day operations.

Is the Free plan really free?

Yes. Free is available forever, with no credit card or trial period. Premium costs ₱499 for 30 days and can be renewed after payment approval.

Can I upgrade later?

Start on Free and request Premium whenever you need the full feature set. Premium starts or renews after your payment is verified.

What is included in each plan?

Free covers the POS, wash queue, services, add-ons, receipts, customer records, and daily sales. Premium adds bookings, staff management, inventory, expenses, reports, loyalty, analytics, multiple branches, and advanced controls.

Can my staff use WashPilot?

Yes. The workspace includes focused roles for owners, cashiers, washers, and supervisors.
